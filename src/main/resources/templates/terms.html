2. What Dokezo does

Dokezo scans issues of the Kenya Gazette for the keywords you choose, such as your name, a title or LR number, a plot number or an ID number. When we find a possible match, we send you the relevant Gazette page with the keyword highlighted, by email and SMS.

  • We are not part of, endorsed by, or affiliated with the Government of Kenya, the Government Printer or the Kenya Gazette.

4. No guarantee of detection

Gazette pages are scanned documents, and reading them automatically is not perfect. A keyword may be misread, a page may be unreadable, an issue may be processed after a delay, and some notices may not be detected at all. Name matching is tolerant of small spelling differences and may produce false alerts. Title, LR and plot numbers are matched exactly, so a notice that prints a number differently from the way you entered it may not match. You should not rely on Dokezo as your only safeguard.
