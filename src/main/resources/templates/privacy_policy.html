1. Introduction & controller details

This policy explains what personal data Dokezo collects, why, and what your rights are. We handle your data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. The data controller is [Company Legal Name], [physical address], Kenya. Our data protection contact is [name or role], privacy@dokezo.co.ke.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address, phone number and a hashed password.
  • National ID number (optional): if you choose to provide it, to help verify records and to use as a keyword.
  • Keywords: the names, title or LR numbers, plot numbers and ID numbers you ask us to watch for.
  • Payment records: the amount, date, M-Pesa phone number and M-Pesa receipt number. We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN or card details.
  • Alert records: which Gazette page matched which keyword, and when and how we notified you.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address, browser type and login times, used for security and troubleshooting.

3. Gazette content

The Kenya Gazette is a public document. We process its content (including names and land details that appear in it) only to match it against the keywords that our clients ask us to watch. We store the Gazette pages we process.

4. Why we use your data

  • To provide the service: scanning the Gazette and alerting you.
  • To create your account, process your payments and send you service messages such as renewal reminders.
  • To keep the service secure and to prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To comply with the law.

Our legal bases are your consent, the performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in running a secure service, and legal obligation. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who help us run Dokezo, and only as much as they need:

  • Payment processing: Safaricom (M-Pesa) [and any other payment provider].
  • SMS delivery: [SMS provider name].
  • Email delivery: [email provider name].
  • Hosting and storage: [hosting provider name and country].

We may also disclose data where required by law or a court order. If any provider processes your data outside Kenya, we take steps to make sure it is protected as the Data Protection Act requires.

6. Alert links and messages

Alerts contain the Gazette page that matched your keyword. SMS alerts contain a private, expiring link rather than the page itself. Anyone who has your phone or your email inbox could see your alerts, so please keep them secure.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and keyword data: while your account is active, and for [period] after it closes.
  • Payment and alert records: for [period], to meet accounting and legal requirements.
  • Technical logs: for [period].

After that, we delete or anonymise the data.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including hashed passwords, encrypted connections and restricted staff access. No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • object to or restrict some uses of your data;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, subject to records we must keep by law; and
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@dokezo.co.ke. We may need to verify your identity first. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

10. Cookies

Dokezo uses only the cookies needed to keep you logged in and secure. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Children

Dokezo is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes by email or SMS, and the date at the top of this page will change.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@dokezo.co.ke or see our contact page.
