The problem: nobody reads the Gazette

Notices about lost title deeds and land transactions are published in the Kenya Gazette. Fraudsters can exploit this: a title is falsely reported lost, a replacement is processed, and the real owner only finds out after the damage is done. The notice was public the whole time — but hundreds of pages a week is more than anyone can watch.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Kenya Gazette?

It’s the government’s official publication where legal notices are announced, including notices about lost land title documents.

Does Dokezo stop a fraudulent transfer?

No. Dokezo gives you early warning so you can act quickly, for example by contacting the Lands registry or a lawyer. It does not replace legal action.

Could a notice be missed?

Gazette pages are scanned documents, so reading them is not perfect. We review matches and use tolerant matching for names, but no automated service can promise it will catch every mention.
