The Problem

Nobody reads the Gazette

Land fraud hiding in plain sight

Notices about lost title deeds and land transactions are published in the Kenya Gazette. Fraudsters exploit this: a title is falsely reported lost, a replacement is processed, and the real owner only finds out after the damage is done. The notice was public the whole time — but hundreds of pages a week is more than anyone can watch.
