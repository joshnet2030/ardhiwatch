THE KENYA GAZETTE 11th September, 2026 4218 (Page 42)

GAZETTE NOTICE NO. 11408

THE LAND REGISTRATION ACT
(No. 3 of 2012)
ISSUE OF A PROVISIONAL CERTIFICATE

WHEREAS Peter Mwangi, of P.O. Box 14820–00100, Nairobi in the Republic of Kenya, is registered as proprietor of all that piece of land known as LR No. 209/1145, situate in the City of Nairobi in the Nairobi Area, and whereas sufficient evidence has been adduced to show that the certificate of title issued thereof has been lost or destroyed, notice is given that after the expiration of sixty (60) days from the date hereof, I shall issue a provisional certificate of title provided that no objection has been received within that period.

Dated the 4th September, 2026.
S. K. MURIUKI,
Registrar of Titles, Nairobi.

Recommended Protective Steps

If you did not apply for a replacement title deed for LR No. 209/1145, act within the statutory 60-day objection window (before 10 Nov 2026):

  1. Check your original title deed to confirm it is safely in your possession.
  2. Instruct your advocate immediately to lodge a formal written objection and place a caveat/restriction with the Registrar of Titles, Nairobi.
  3. Visit Ardhi House / Ardhisasa with your original title and National ID to flag the fraudulent application.
